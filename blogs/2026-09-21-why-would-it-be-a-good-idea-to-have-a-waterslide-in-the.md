Building a chaired multi-agent debate as a Claude Code skill Ask a language model a hard question and you get one answer, and the only person who will challenge it is you. Ask it six times with six different personas and you get six essays, plus a summary that smooths over everything interesting. When we started using Claude Code for design decisions and plans rather than for code, that second failure was the one that bothered us: the disagreements were where the value was, and the summary was where they disappeared.
